    How it's measured

    Every day each AI model gets the same questions about cafés in Korea, in English and Korean, through its API. A chain counts as mentioned when its name appears in the answer.

    Accuracy

    Checked against 21 answers labeled by hand: every chain the tracker reported was right, and it found 95.6% of the chains actually mentioned.

    Limits

    Answers from an API can differ from what people see in the apps. Change compares the same questions on both dates. Logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify each brand.
